Space Travel

Ken Thompson, PDP-7, 1969. Ported to Rust.

You start parked on Earth. The ship is always at the centre of the crosshair, and the view turns with it. At first Earth is too small to see, so the screen looks empty.

  1. Zoom in with ++ about 8 times: Earth's outline appears. The number at the bottom is the zoom.
  2. Take off: zoom in to about −18, then hold ▼↓. Thrust gets stronger as you zoom out, so stay zoomed in for gentle flying.
  3. Steer with ⟲ ⟳← →; ▲↑ thrusts the other way.
  4. Land on any planet or moon slower than about 1 km/s. L means landed, CL means you crashed: press RESETr to clear it and take off again from there.
  5. Lost? NEWn starts over, parked on Earth.

Zoom out to find the Moon, the Sun and the planets. The name at the bottom left is the body whose gravity you are in.
